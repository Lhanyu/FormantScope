# FormantScope Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** April 30, 2026

FormantScope (“the App”) is developed by Yu Ran Wang. This policy describes how the App handles information on your device.

## Summary

- The App **uses the microphone** only to analyze sound **in real time** on your device (pitch and formant estimates, and level for display).
- **We do not operate servers** that receive your voice audio for this App. Processing is done **locally** on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac.
- **Optional** feature: you may **save recordings** to a **folder you choose** on your device (or connected storage). Those files **stay under your control**; we do not upload them.

## Microphone

When you tap **Start Listening**, the App accesses the microphone to compute and display acoustic measurements (e.g. fundamental frequency and formants). The audio is used **only on-device** to drive the analysis and UI. We **do not** collect, store, or transmit your microphone audio to the developer.

## Optional WAV export

If you use **Record**, the App may write a **WAV file** to the **recording folder you selected** in Settings (using system file access on iOS, or a folder you pick on macOS). The developer **does not** receive these files. You can delete them anytime in the Files app or in Finder.

## Data we do not collect

The App **does not** require an account. We do **not** intentionally collect:

- Your name, email, or Apple ID through the App  
- Analytics or advertising identifiers for this App  
- Your voice recordings on our servers (we have none for this purpose)

If Apple’s systems process minimal technical data when you download or update the App from the App Store, that is subject to [Apple’s Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Third-party code

The App uses **open-source audio libraries** (e.g. via Swift Package Manager) to perform audio I/O and analysis. They run **on your device** like the rest of the App. See the project repository for licenses.

## Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. Parents and guardians should supervise microphone use as they see fit.

## Changes

We may update this policy if the App’s behavior changes. The **Latest updated** date at the top will change; continued use of the App after updates means you accept the revised policy.
